Quick start This is a working demo of the simulation planned for your CTC-021. It runs entirely in your browser.

Watch it run itself

Turn Line Power Transfer (bottom right of the panel) to 2. The computer dispatcher takes over: trains enter, routes are lined and meets happen at the sidings. Use 10× or 30× at the top to speed things up.

Dispatch it yourself

Turn Line Power Transfer to 1. Click a lever plate's left or right side to move a switch or signal lever, then press that column's code button. The lamps only change once the field answers back over the code line, about 5 to 10 seconds later.

Things to try

Line a train into a siding for a meet. Cancel a signal in front of a train and the red lamp flashes for two minutes of time locking. Flip a model board toggle to fail a track circuit, or use Instructor below to enter trains.
